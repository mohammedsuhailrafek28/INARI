Third-party dependencies keep their own licenses (see `pnpm-lock.yaml`).

## Data policy for this repository
- Only synthetic or sanitised financial data may be committed or used in the public demo.
- Never commit credentials. If a credential is ever committed, rotate it and report it; do not rely on deleting the file.
- Anonymous per-browser demo identity (planned) is a demo isolation mechanism, **not** production authentication.

## Third-party data
- `data/parser-tests/sms/indian-payment-sms-dataset.json` — "Indian Payment SMS Corpus" v0.2.0 by the SplitEasy contributors (https://github.com/skyaara/indian-payment-sms-dataset), CC BY 4.0, unmodified. See `data/parser-tests/sms/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
- `data/demo/*` is generated by this repository's own seeded generator (fictional data). The idea of merchant templates is inspired by esaipavan/synthetic-finance-data (MIT); no code or data copied.
- `pdfjs-dist` (Mozilla PDF.js, Apache-2.0, https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js) is used unmodified as a dependency to read the text layer of digital PDF statements. `data/parser-tests/pdf/*` is synthetic and generated by this repository's own `data/generator/make_pdf_statements.py` (uses `reportlab` and `pypdf` at generation time only; neither is a runtime dependency).
- FinEE (Apache-2.0) and DylanOrazi/Personal-Finance-Spending-Dashboard (no licence) are **not** included; see `data/training/README.md` and `data/external-eval/README.md`.
